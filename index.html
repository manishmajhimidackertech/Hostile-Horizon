HOW TO PLAY

Fly through each sortie, shoot down enemy aircraft and bomb ground targets. Survive to the end and destroy the heavy target to win. Spend credits in the hangar on upgrades.

  • Cannons fire where your nose points. Dive to strafe ground targets.
  • Missiles lock onto the nearest aircraft. They can also shoot down enemy rockets.
  • Bombs keep your forward speed. Release them just before you pass over tanks and flak guns.
  • Chain kills quickly to build a combo score multiplier.
  • Pick up crates. Green crates repair your hull and olive crates restock ammo.
  • Install: use your browser's “Install app” option, or on iOS use Share → Add to Home Screen. The game works offline once installed.
